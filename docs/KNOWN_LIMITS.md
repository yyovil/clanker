# Known limits

- **Payments.** Clanker cannot complete online payments today. Card enrollment may succeed, but end-to-end paid checkout is not verified, especially for Indian merchant flows.

- **Team credential sharing.** Inviting workspace members to use shared credentials, and the admin controls around that, work in principle but the UX is brittle. Coworking that depends on smooth share/revoke flows is not polished yet.

- **Response style.** Replies are often not concise or pointed enough. The assistant still surfaces more process and detail than the intended Slack UX.
